Privacy Policy — SDR Spectrum Analyzer

Last Updated: September 2026
Application: SDR Spectrum Analyzer
Publisher: Mattino 507 Software

1. Overview

SDR Spectrum Analyzer is a Windows desktop application published by Mattino 507 Software. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through our compliance with this policy.

2. Information Collection and Use

Mattino 507 Software does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information through SDR Spectrum Analyzer. The application:

  • Does not require a separate Mattino 507 Software account or login credentials.
  • Does not contain advertising.
  • Does not use analytics, telemetry, or cloud-based tracking services.

SDR Spectrum Analyzer may communicate with Microsoft Store services to determine product licensing and, when requested by the user, to offer an optional in-app purchase. Microsoft Store account, licensing, and payment information are handled by Microsoft and are not collected or stored by Mattino 507 Software.

3. Local Data Processing

Radio-frequency (RF) sample data and measurement results are processed strictly locally on the user's computer. SDR Spectrum Analyzer does not transmit RF measurement data or usage statistics to Mattino 507 Software or to any third-party online services.

4. Hardware Interaction

The application may access locally connected compatible USB software-defined radio (SDR) hardware in order to perform its intended spectrum-analysis functions. Hardware identification and operational data required by the application are processed entirely locally.

5. Files Created and Stored by User

If the user exports measurement data, screenshots, or other files, those files are saved in file system locations directly selected by the user and remain entirely under the user's control.

6. Third-Party Components

SDR Spectrum Analyzer includes third-party open-source or runtime software components required for local operation. These components operate locally and do not transmit personal information to Mattino 507 Software or external servers.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time if the application's functionality or data-handling practices change. Any updates will be posted with a revised "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.

8. Contact Information

Questions or comments about this Privacy Policy may be submitted through the SDR Spectrum Analyzer support page: SDR Spectrum Analyzer Support.
